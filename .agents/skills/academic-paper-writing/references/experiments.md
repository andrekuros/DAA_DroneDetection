# Experiments

## Goal

Protocol first; then results ordered by the paper story (diagnostics before architecture fights if that is the thesis).

## Structure

1. Protocol (N, seeds, CI/test, primary contrast)
2. Main positive result tables/figures
3. Controls / ablations / honest negatives
4. Boundary suites or limitations-as-experiments

## Tables and figures

- Minimal-ink tables; units in headers; N in caption
- Captions describe content, do not argue the claim
- Pair Δ + CI for primary contrasts; do not say “significant” if CI crosses 0
- Grayscale-readable plots; labeled axes

## Fairness

Same observability mask, replan cadence, and evaluation score across compared Local methods.

## Avoid

Editorial captions (“claim fails”); burying the Global oracle; architecture claims without matched non-attention / non-GNN controls.
