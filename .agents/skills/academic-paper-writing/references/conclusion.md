# Conclusion

## Goal

Restate the scoped claim and boundaries in a few sentences. No new results.

## Mini-outline

1. What was shown (metric, baseline, conditions)
2. What was *not* shown (architecture, oracle, hardware, …)
3. One sentence implication for the community / C2 practice if warranted

## Avoid

Future-work wish lists as contributions; repeating the entire experiments section; stronger claims than the abstract.
